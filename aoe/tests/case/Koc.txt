Koç University Pub Placement — Stochastic Optimization Problem

PROBLEM DESCRIPTION:

Koç University is planning to open exactly 3 student pubs on its Rumelifeneri campus
to maximize expected daily revenue. The university has identified 8 strategic candidate
locations for pub placement:

1. Sports Hall Hub (near athletic facilities)
2. Engineering Faculty (central academic building)
3. Science Faculty (high-traffic area)
4. Library Central (student hub)
5. CASE Business School (business faculty)
6. Humanities Hub (arts and sciences)
7. North Dorm Cluster (residential area)
8. South Dorm Cluster (residential area)

CAMPUS BUILDINGS AND DEMAND:

The campus has 38 buildings classified into two categories:
  - Dormitories (residence halls): A Yurdu, B Yurdu, C Yurdu, D Yurdu, E Yurdu,
    F Yurdu, G Yurdu, H Yurdu, I Yurdu, J Yurdu, K Yurdu, L Yurdu, M Yurdu,
    N Yurdu, O Yurdu, P Yurdu, R Yurdu, T Yurdu, S Yurdu, Kışba, Henry Ford (24 dorms total)
  - Academic/Other buildings (faculties, libraries, admin, dining, sports):
    Spor Salonu, Fen Fakültesi, Mühendislik Fakültesi, HUMS Fakültesi, Hukuk Fakültesi,
    CASE Fakültesi, Kütüphane, ELC, SGKM, and others (14 buildings)

The daily number of people from each building who are interested in visiting a pub is
uncertain and follows a known probability distribution (we have historical data with
mean and standard deviation for each building).

DISTANCE DECAY MODEL:

A person's probability of visiting a pub depends on the distance from their building:
  - For dormitory residents: visit probability decreases by 0.5% per meter
  - For academic/other buildings: visit probability decreases by 0.3% per meter

Each person will visit the nearest open pub if the visit probability is positive.

PARAMETERS AND CONSTRAINTS:

Each candidate location has:
  - A fixed opening cost (one-time investment)
  - A maximum daily customer capacity

The university has:
  - A total capital budget for opening costs
  - A revenue of 45 TL per customer visit
  - Demand modeled with 10 stochastic scenarios (sampled from normal distributions)

OBJECTIVES AND CONSTRAINTS:

Maximize: Expected total daily revenue across all demand scenarios
Subject to:
  1. Exactly 3 pubs must be opened from the 8 candidate locations
  2. Each building's population is assigned to at most one pub (nearest open one)
  3. Buildings can only be assigned to pubs that are actually open
  4. Each pub's capacity cannot be exceeded in any demand scenario
  5. Total fixed opening costs must not exceed the available budget

DATA FILES:

All parameter data will be provided in CSV files:
  - buildings.csv: building names, types, and demand distributions (mean, std dev)
  - candidate_locations.csv: pub locations, costs, and capacities
  - distance_matrix.csv: distances from each building to each candidate location (meters)
  - demand_scenarios.csv: 10 pre-sampled demand scenarios (for reproducible testing)
  - global_parameters.csv: budget, revenue, decay rates, and number of scenarios
